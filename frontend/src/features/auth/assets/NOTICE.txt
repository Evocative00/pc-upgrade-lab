https://developers.naver.com/inc/devcenter/downloads/bi/NAVER_login_KR.ai
https://developers.naver.com/docs/login/bi/bi.md

These marks identify their respective sign-in providers. They remain
the brand assets of Google, Kakao and NAVER, not project-owned logos.
