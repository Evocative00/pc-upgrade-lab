# Public shared catalog inputs

Contains data from [BuildCores OpenDB](https://github.com/buildcores/buildcores-open-db), licensed under [ODC-By-1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/by/1-0/). The original source records, revisions and license copies are retained in `backend/src/main/resources/catalog/seed/`.

The 307 source identities combine the reviewed 300-product seed and seven manufacturer-sourced pilot products. Canonical sharing does not promote verification, activation, model or sale-variant classification. Manufacturer review evidence is preserved in `data/catalog-review/`.

The original 74-price baseline is derived from the reviewed files in `data/catalog-current-prices/` and the eight pilot observations, replacing six older observations. Provider URLs and original observation timestamps are preserved.

`active-approved-prices.json` contains the current 80-price bundle approved and published on 2026-10-10. The initial 75 prices comprise 72 reobserved product prices, two preserved Samsung SSD observations, and one newly reviewed Dell S2721DGF observation. A subsequent approval added five reviewed observations (three CPUs, one GPU and one motherboard), preserving all previous 75 prices and all 307 identities. The [publication and production verification record](../catalog-review/remaining232-price-publication-2026-10-10.json) records this price-only redeployment; no database, product specifications, environment variables, team token or backend restart changed. Prices are domestic NEW product amounts, excluding shipping and conditional promotions. Future proposal files require review before deployment; this approval covers only the published 80-price bundle.

The latest provider also uses `approved-retail-extension-2026-10-10.json` for the approved 308-product/81-observed-price catalog, while preserving the legacy 307-product/80-price inputs above. `active-reference-prices.json` supplies 227 separately approved references: five model-level retail references, three official launch prices, and 219 similar-part estimates. Their provenance, unit basis, dates and ranges remain distinct from observed product prices. `reference-prices-preview-2026-10-10.json` is the original unpublished review; the active copy differs only in its publication approval flag. See the [reference publication verification](../catalog-review/reference-price-publication-2026-10-10.json).
