The buildcores directory contains eight original JSON records from that revision.
The manifest contains PC Upgrade Lab's normalized catalog fields and curated
manufacturer specification extracts. These extracts are not copies of the
manufacturer's full pages and are explicitly labeled CURATED_SPEC_EXTRACT.
Official source URLs and retrieval times are included with every record.
Normalized values may differ from the source records where manufacturer evidence
was used, or remain null when not confirmed. No compatibility or price verification
is implied. Manufacturer trademarks belong to their respective owners.

When displaying or redistributing this dataset or material derived from it, retain
the BuildCores attribution and license reference. A public catalog UI must also
expose appropriate attribution to its users.

This batch adds eight exact desktop graphics-card models: four NVIDIA-chip cards,
three AMD-chip cards and one Intel-chip card. Card power and its published basis
remain distinct from the complete-system minimum/recommended PSU wattage.
The RX 7600's missing 8-pin connector and incorrect slot count are corrected using
the manufacturer page. Unknown dimension axes, physical connector lanes and slot
occupancy are not inferred. The RTX 5070's unconfirmed 16-pin subtype remains
PCIE_16PIN_UNSPECIFIED and its incorrect WHITE alias is not adopted.
The Arc A750 card power remains unknown; an Intel reference-chip rating is not
used as a confirmed value for this ASRock board. Its exact manufacturer model
code is used instead of the concatenated raw alias. Existing seed batches,
reviewed product states, source rows and reference prices are preserved.
