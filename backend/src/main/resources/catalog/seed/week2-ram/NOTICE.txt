The buildcores directory contains eight original JSON records from that revision.
The manifest contains PC Upgrade Lab's normalized catalog fields and curated
manufacturer specification extracts. These extracts are not copies of the
manufacturer's full pages and are explicitly labeled CURATED_SPEC_EXTRACT.
Official source URLs and retrieval times are included with every record.
Normalized values may differ from the source records where manufacturer evidence
was used, or remain null when not confirmed. No compatibility or price verification
is implied. Manufacturer trademarks belong to their respective owners.

When displaying or redistributing this dataset or material derived from it, retain
the BuildCores attribution and license reference. A future public catalog UI must
also expose appropriate attribution to its users.

This batch adds eight desktop, non-ECC unbuffered DIMM products. Six contain
one physical module and two are kits of two modules. Capacity is stored per
module; profile data rate/voltage and default JEDEC settings remain distinct.
Manufacturer evidence corrects the DDR5-5200 profile voltage. Unverified aliases
and replacement SKUs are not adopted. Existing batches and prices are preserved.
